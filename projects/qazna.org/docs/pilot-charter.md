# Shadow food-provisioning pilot charter

Status: proposed operational charter, 4 November 2026. The author delegated pilot
scope and the modelling choice of basic-provision funding. No real participant,
organization, funds or production deployment has been enrolled by this document.

## Scope and principles

Start with one consenting community, one food provider, and independently assigned
inspectors. Qazna maintains parallel records and operator-reviewed proposals.
Existing supply remains authoritative during the shadow pilot. People can refuse
or leave without losing ordinary support. The organism analogy means needs,
feedback, reserves and repair; it does not imply obedience or moral ranking.

Pilot success is not adoption of a spiritual or historical thesis. Governance must
protect disagreement and require accountability from administrators too. External
supply disruption and internal privilege capture are distinct scenarios to test,
not predictions of inevitable failure of a country.

## Selected experimental funding rule

1. Record a need even when the participant has no accounting balance.
2. Establish physically available, suitable and attested stock before promising it.
3. The operator calculates the exact accounting shortfall for the proposed provision.
4. Allocate the shortfall from a pre-authorized, finite treasury reserve. Record
   period, category and basis. Use existing allocation/transfer and reservation
   controls; neither a model nor a shortage event issues measure.
5. If the reserve is insufficient, expose a treasury shortage separately from
   physical scarcity and route the case to the human continuity process. A failed
   software allocation must not silently cancel the person's need or existing aid.
6. Receipt still requires the recipient and custodian, then operator settlement.

The executable strict HTTP exercise demonstrates a zero-balance recipient,
attested stock, rejection of an unaffordable treasury allocation, a successful exact
shortfall allocation, and confirmed delivery. It is not a general automatic welfare
policy. The current one-allocation-per-resident/period/category limit and the lack
of automatic replenishment/compensation require explicit handling before a live
pilot. Allocation and reservation are separate operator commands; if stock changes
between them, the allocation remains a recorded balance, not a false receipt.

Treasury reserve authorization, circulating measure returning from providers,
repeat support and budget ceilings need versioned rules before consequential use.
No new issuance endpoint is authorized or implemented here.

## Scarcity policy to validate

For the initial shadow comparison, evaluate equal proportional coverage of the
same declared food category/unit, with a published deterministic remainder rule.
Compare it with the existing caller-order planner and report who waits and why.
Medical urgency or age-specific quantities require qualified human-approved norms;
do not infer them from a model or synthetic actor age. This comparison is a proposed
evaluation, not an implemented change to the production planner.

## Preconditions before real participation

Name the responsible organization, human contact and independent appeal reviewer;
obtain voluntary participation; determine the actual resources, needs and baseline;
set privacy/retention and evidence-custody rules; implement identity/ownership,
revocation/replacement, disputes/corrections and continuity safeguards; define and
rehearse incident response. Do not infer these approvals from repository access.

Set numerical acceptance thresholds and observation duration before enrollment.
Measure verified delivered coverage, waiting time, errors/exclusion, waste,
administrative effort, successful appeals, outages and operating cost against an
agreed baseline. Stop on unauthorized access, unverifiable material discrepancy,
loss of reliable history, or harmful dependence on the shadow system. Preserve the
failure evidence and maintain ordinary provision.

## Deliverables and open dependencies

The [AI economic research protocol](ai-economic-research.md#experiment-design)
defines the proposed comparison: identical resources and information across
policies, non-AI baselines, independently measured delivery, subgroup outcomes,
total review/correction effort, choice and privacy. Separate policy effects from
AI effects. Do not remove existing support to create a control group. A shadow
pilot alone does not establish causal or national-scale effects; pre-register
outcomes and use an independent evaluator before making effectiveness claims.

- A reproducible synthetic demonstration and recorded failure cases.
- A participant-facing explanation and an accessible assisted path.
- A rights/authority matrix and a reviewed budget/continuity procedure.
- Evidence of restoration and restricted privileges, plus a real deployment review.
- Independent pilot evaluation before broader categories or multi-site operation.

See [completion roadmap](completion-roadmap.md) for work still open. This charter
is not a claim of legal approval, societal readiness, or national economic validity.
