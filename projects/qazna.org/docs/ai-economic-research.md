## Research question

Can needs-based resource coordination preserve essential provision and personal choice during AI-driven changes in work, while reducing exclusion, waste and administrative burden?

The proposed contribution is an executable way to test this question. Qazna separates physical resources, circulating accounting measure, forecasting and accountable decisions. It does not condition basic needs on employment or productivity. Interest, speculative returns, taxes and deposits are not being added as funding mechanisms. Practical provisioning, maintenance and staff still require real resources; an accounting transfer does not create them.

The full book supplies the author's motivation and wider economic proposal. This brief makes a bounded part independently testable. Evidence for that part would not, by itself, establish the historical hypotheses or validate a replacement for a national monetary system.

## What the four organizations are investigating

### Anthropic: transitions and continuity of basic provision

The July 2026 [Economic Futures Research Fund agenda](https://www.anthropic.com/news/economic-futures-research-fund-agenda) includes workplace organization, transition assistance, durable income support, shared gains and public services. It explicitly includes basic-needs relief and prolonged unconditional support. Its global fund targets institutional projects, normally $5–30 million, with no direct awards below $1 million. Eligible applicants include universities, research/policy institutes and experienced experimental nonprofits, not individuals applying personally. These are announced terms, not Qazna eligibility or an award offer.

Our inference: this is the closest substantive match for testing continuity of provision independently of employment. Qazna's distinct proposal is to coordinate actual needs and capacities as well as accounting. The agenda also considers taxes and capital/dividend instruments; those are comparison policies, not mechanisms silently adopted by Qazna. An eligible institution with field-evaluation experience would be a more credible lead than presenting the current software as a large ready-made intervention.

The January [Economic Index report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report) distinguishes task complexity, autonomy, skill and success in observed Claude use. Its October [expectations study](https://www.anthropic.com/research/ai-expectations-gap) compares US adults' and Anthropic staff's beliefs about future capabilities and work; their expectations differ substantially. Platform use and expectations are different evidence classes. Neither is a measured future unemployment rate. Use them to construct sensitivity ranges, never to assign an individual's entitlement or treat one forecast as certain.

## Measurement contract

The following equations are proposed evaluation definitions. They are not new runtime policy or claims of available dashboards.

For person `i`, category/unit `k` and period `t`, let `n_ikt > 0` be a frozen, validated need and `r_ikt` suitable confirmed receipt by its deadline. Count only evidence meeting the evaluation protocol:

```text
coverage_ikt = min(r_ikt, n_ikt) / n_ikt
unmet_ikt    = max(n_ikt - r_ikt, 0)
coverage_kt  = mean_i(coverage_ikt) over positive-need participants
```

Report population coverage, category-specific unmet quantities and worst-group coverage separately. Never add litres, meals and kilowatt-hours. Cap coverage so excess receipt for one participant cannot hide another's deprivation. A zero need is not a 100%-covered observation. Preserve both original and corrected need versions; an administrator must not improve the metric by deleting an unmet need. Missing receipt evidence is unknown, not automatically delivered: publish missingness and best/worst bounds. Employment, AI use and contribution are explanatory variables, never prerequisites for basic provision.

An illustrative arithmetic check: three people need 100 same-unit meals each, with 240 available. Caller-order allocation `(100,100,40)` and proportional allocation `(80,80,80)` both give mean coverage `0.8`; minimum coverage changes from `0.4` to `0.8`. This demonstrates why an average alone is inadequate. It does not prove proportional allocation is medically suitable or that Qazna improves production. The proportional allocator is not implemented yet.

Physical feasibility must survive every period and location:

```text
next_stock = current_stock + usable_output + arrivals
             - process_inputs - delivered - dispatches - spoilage
next_stock >= 0
planned_capacity_use <= actually_available_capacity
```

Transfers must match dispatch/arrival records with realistic travel time; forecasts cannot be counted as usable output. Validate unit consistency, input competition and delayed production independently of the planner. Separate scenarios for physical scarcity and accounting liquidity. The monetary check is:

```text
sum(account_balances_next) = sum(account_balances_now)
                            + explicitly_authorized_issuance
                            - explicitly_authorized_retirement
```

Normal transfer-only periods set the last two terms to zero. This equation specifies an audit invariant, not permission to add issuance or retirement endpoints. Trace continuing household/provider/worker/input/maintenance circulation across periods; report reserves and shortages without silently topping up a scenario.

Measure total staff time as registration + planning + review + correction + inspection + support. Report time per completed case together with failures, repeat visits and participant time. A faster interface that increases harmful errors fails the proposed objective. Measure learning with later unaided tasks, not output while AI is available. Measure choice through acceptable options, refusal without loss of ordinary support, and appeal outcomes; no moral or productivity ranking of people.

## Experiment design

### Stage A: reproducible synthetic and historical replay

Build the research evaluator in Rust, with deterministic seeds, exact accounting and an independent reference model. Earlier Go mechanisms, if adopted, must be rewritten and revalidated. Python may inspect/export results under the existing non-authoritative boundary. No live participant data or model call is required for the baseline suite.

Compare three distinct interventions using identical needs, initial resources, authorized accounting measure, capacity, arrivals and information availability:

1. Current documented caller-order planner.
2. A published proportional/urgency policy approved for comparison, implemented independently.
3. The same chosen policy with an independently validated optimization or AI-assisted workflow.

This separates the effect of a policy change from the effect of AI. Keep an offline, non-AI baseline. Preserve the same actor choices and disruption seeds across comparisons. Do not make a baseline artificially incompetent, or give Qazna future information that the baseline cannot see.

Scenario dimensions include no displacement, temporary task changes and sustained employment loss; capacity growth versus no growth; correlated supply interruption; input/energy bottlenecks; conflicting needs; privacy restrictions; language/access barriers; delayed feedback; provider outage; and captured or mistaken administrators. Severity values are explicit experimental inputs, not forecasts inferred from adoption percentages. Model household demand, staffing and production separately so loss of paid employment is not mechanically equated with loss of all productive capacity.

Use public aggregate AI-use research only after checking dataset permissions, sampling, release date, geography and task definitions. Never infer an individual's unemployment, competence or need from a platform's aggregate task mix. Publish source versions, transformations and uncertainty ranges. Synthetic agent economies can reveal software/policy failure modes; they cannot establish human consent or causal household welfare gains.

### Stage B: voluntary shadow operation

Follow the [pilot charter](pilot-charter.md). Existing provision continues; no person is denied essentials to create an experimental control. Compare observed workflows and shadow proposals using an agreed baseline. Freeze eligibility/need definitions, deadlines, outcomes, subgroups, exclusion rules and missing-data handling before evaluation. One willing community is a feasibility study, not a nationally representative trial.

Recruit an independent evaluator and an accountable operator. Record protocol departures, refusals, dropouts, extra staff assistance and all operating inputs. If causal claims are intended, use a justified assignment strategy with appropriate review: for example, randomizing nonessential planning assistance across teams while preserving actual support. Shared suppliers create interference; use an appropriate cluster design or model spillovers explicitly. A before/after comparison alone cannot isolate AI effects from staffing, seasonality or supply changes.

Choose sample size only after specifying a primary outcome, baseline variability, minimal meaningful effect, clustering, expected attrition and error rates. Do not pretend six synthetic actors or a large number of transactions provide that statistical power. Pre-register the analysis and publish confidence intervals, adverse outcomes and null results.

### Decision rules

An economic-success claim requires better predeclared coverage/continuity outcomes at comparable real inputs, without unacceptable loss of choice, privacy or subgroup access. Productivity requires total time/cost accounting, not just fast model output. Numeric thresholds must be agreed before enrollment; they are intentionally not invented here.

Unauthorized economic actions, corrupted histories, harmful dependence, privacy breaches or unresolvable material discrepancies stop the affected experiment and activate ordinary provision/incident procedures. A failed hypothesis is a valid research result. Keep software test success separate from empirical intervention success.

### Compact English research brief

**Working title:** AI-optional coordination of essential provision under employment disruption.

**Research question:** Can deterministic resource planning with exact accounting preserve provision and human choice through workforce and supply changes, and when does optional AI assistance improve outcomes enough to justify its full operating and oversight costs?

**Contribution:** Qazna is an open Rust/PostgreSQL research prototype. It registers needs independently of employment, separates physical shortages from accounting liquidity, constructs reviewable provisioning drafts and preserves append-only accounting. The authoritative calculation and application path does not require an AI provider. A small exact integer reference demonstrates where optimization improves over sequential allocation; it is not yet a global runtime planner.

**Study:** Compare identical-resource, identical-policy scenarios with (a) the deterministic baseline, (b) mathematical optimization and (c) the same optimized workflow plus bounded AI assistance. Freeze information availability and disruption seeds. Report provision coverage and worst-group outcomes, physical/accounting feasibility, unmet quantities, time to recovery, full staff effort, choice/appeals and total cost. Evaluate no-model, timeout, misleading-advice and security-event scenarios. Later voluntary shadow operation requires an independent institutional evaluator and a willing service provider; those partners are not yet established.

**Current evidence and limitations:** Offline accounting/provisioning tests, an exact small optimization reference, editable cost assumptions and a published architecture review. No claim of national readiness, empirical unemployment prediction or proven superiority of the proposed economy. Present cost figures are scenarios; command throughput, solver scaling, staff workload and participant outcomes remain to be measured.

**Requested collaboration:** Methodological criticism; identification of an eligible research lead or programme; co-design of an independently evaluated, privacy-preserving shadow study. Financial support, API credits and restricted data are not assumed. The project welcomes a finding that a simpler non-AI workflow is preferable.

**Outputs:** Reproducible Rust scenarios, independently checked balances, a public protocol, measured AI/non-AI comparisons, documented failure regimes and a report including null results. Funding arrangements must preserve independent publication and accurate attribution.

**Links:** [Active source and documentation](https://github.com/DauletBai/tartarus/tree/codex/qazna-rust-provisioning/projects/qazna.org); [full Russian working book](https://dauletbai.github.io/tartarus/). The book is unpublished and still being developed. Its broader arguments provide the author's motivation; accepting a technical collaboration does not require endorsing its historical or linguistic hypotheses.

### Proposed role, value and honest boundaries

Propose Qazna Technologies as an engineering and field-implementation partner in
an independently evaluated Kazakhstan study. A university/research organization
should own the research design and evaluation; a consenting service provider
should own operational continuity; participants should retain choice and appeal.
Astana Hub can be asked for introductions and programme guidance. No institution
may be named as a partner before it agrees.

The project can offer a needs-first experimental specification, exact Rust
accounting, resource/production constraints, replayable scenarios and a comparison
that can find AI unnecessary. Its optional-model architecture is valuable for
isolating AI's contribution, even if it is less attractive as a model-consumption
sales opportunity. A vendor's research team and its sales or venture team have
different incentives.

The organism analogy is a design motivation. It is not proof of predictive
certainty, perfect delivery, absence of corruption or macroeconomic equilibrium.
The current planner accepts at most 32 needs and 64 candidates per proposal and
uses caller ordering; national throughput and globally optimal allocation have
not been demonstrated. An exact small research example is available separately.
Resident interfaces, disputes/corrections, independent measurement and the
multi-period evaluation still need work before a consequential pilot.

The full unpublished book should remain available alongside a short English
entry document. Its historical and linguistic hypotheses are not premises that
a research partner must accept. Empirical technical results cannot establish
those hypotheses. Criticism should address institutions, mechanisms and evidence,
not attach greed or moral worth to ethnic or religious identity. The pilot must
serve participants without belief or ancestry tests.

### A six-month staged study

1. **Months 1–2: protocol and demonstrator.** Recruit an independent evaluator
   and provider; agree resource category, needs, baseline, units, privacy and stop
   rules. Record existing service costs and variance. Implement missing workflows
   required by the selected scope. Release a repeatable synthetic demonstration.
2. **Months 3–4: parallel operational records.** Use one willing provider and a
   voluntary community. A planning envelope of 100–200 adults is illustrative,
   not an enrolled sample or a statistical power result. Existing provision stays
   authoritative. Reconcile declared resources with observed deliveries and
   record missing evidence as unknown. Do not withdraw support for comparison.
3. **Months 5–6: evaluation and decision.** Replay identical inputs through the
   baseline, mathematical optimizer and optimizer plus optional AI. Assess
   feasibility, minimum-group coverage, waiting, waste, review/correction effort,
   accessibility, outages and complete cost. Any live workflow comparison needs
   its own approved assignment and analysis plan; repeated replay observations
   are not independent households or causal field evidence.

A small shadow study can establish operational feasibility and measurement
quality. It cannot prove that unemployment will occur at a specified rate, that
income-independent support works nationwide, or that all taxes/banks can be
replaced. Later institution-led studies would have to evaluate actual provision,
funding continuity, incentives, long-term wellbeing and transition effects.
Agree measurable success and stopping thresholds after baseline collection and
before outcome analysis, including publication of null and negative results.

## Automation scenarios in the physical graph

Evidence reviewed 11 November 2026. [IFR's 24 September 2026 release](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/five-million-robots-now-operate-in-factories-globally)
reports five million industrial robots operating in 2025 and more than 600,000
annual installations. Its [30 September service-robot release](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/global-sales-of-professional-service-robots-surge-24-percent)
reports almost 250,000 professional shipments, including about 117,000 in transport
and logistics. These observations
support explicitly investigating physical automation; they are not coefficients
for the Qazna deployment scenarios.

[ILO–NASK's 20 May 2025 task-based study](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/one-four-jobs-risk-being-transformed-genai-new-ilo%E2%80%93nask-global-index-shows)
finds potential GenAI exposure in one quarter of employment and describes task
transformation as more likely than wholesale replacement. Exposure is not an
observed unemployment rate or a physical factory productivity forecast. The
[IEA's 2026 assessment](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary)
shows the importance of changing task efficiency, rising uptake and more intensive
AI applications. It supports explicit energy sensitivity, not a universal cost
per robot or per AI inference.

Implementation: the [physical automation annex](physical-circulation.md#automation-seasonal-output-and-dated-distribution--11-november-2026)
compares slow/reference/rapid adoption, commissioning and qualification delays,
shared human/robot capacity, energy and upkeep inputs, planned shutdowns and a
common control outage. Scenario rates are declared synthetic choices. No source
above is presented as validating them. Identical needs remain mandatory; freed
human work is not grounds to remove provision, compel retraining or rank people.
AI can improve future candidate schedules or engineering estimates, but cannot
turn a forecast into equipment, a trained crew or a ledger authorization.
